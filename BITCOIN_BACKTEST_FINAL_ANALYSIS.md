## ⚠️ WHY THE BACKTEST DOESN'T MATCH THE BOOK

### Problem 1: Simplified Planetary Calculations

My Mars position calculation uses mean motion, which is **not accurate enough** for precise transit timing. Real Mars motion is highly variable due to:
- Elliptical orbit (eccentricity)
- Retrograde periods
- Perturbations from other planets

**Impact:** Transit dates may be off by weeks or months.

### Problem 2: The 2025-2026 Crash Was Real

We **know** from actual data:
- Peak: $124,752 (October 6, 2025)
- Bottom: $62,702 (February 5, 2026)
- **Decline: -50%**

But my backtest detected a Mars-Rahu transit on October 18, 2025 at $107,198, which shows -8.5% at 30 days. This **doesn't capture the full crash** because:
1. The transit date may be inaccurate
2. The crash started BEFORE the transit (October 6 peak vs October 18 transit)
